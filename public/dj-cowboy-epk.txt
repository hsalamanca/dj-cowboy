DJ COWBOY — ELECTRONIC PRESS KIT
================================

Austin-rooted house & disco DJ for clubs, private events, and corporate nights.

BIO (short)
-----------
DJ Cowboy brings road-trip energy to the dancefloor — bass-heavy house, dusty disco, and late-night selections built for rooms that want to move.

BIO (long)
----------
Based in Austin, Texas, DJ Cowboy cuts open-range grooves for clubs, weddings, backyard parties, and brand nights. Expect house and disco with a selector’s ear: warm-up sunsets, peak-time pressure, and clean edits that keep the floor locked without losing the plot. Available for Austin metro and regional travel.
